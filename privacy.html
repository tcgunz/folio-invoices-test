Folio privacy policy

Effective November 22, 2026 · Applies to the Folio app for Android and the Folio web app

In short: Folio keeps your documents, receipts, invoices and income on your own device. Folio has no account to sign up for, no servers that store your files, no ads and no tracking. Your information only leaves your device when you choose to send, share or back it up.

Who we are

Folio is made by Tommy Chieng, in Ontario, Canada ("we", "us"). You can reach us at the contact address at the end of this policy.

What Folio keeps, and where

Everything you put into Folio is stored on your device, inside the app (or, for the web app, in your browser's storage):

  • documents and scans you open, edit, sign or save;
  • receipts and the details read from them (store, date, amounts, tax, category, notes);
  • invoices, income records and the client details you type in (name, email, address);
  • your business details, logo, payment details (such as your e-Transfer email) and settings.

In the Android app, Folio also keeps a copy of your documents and receipts in the Documents › Folio folder on your phone so you can find them in your Files app.

We don't collect, receive or have access to any of this information.

What Folio does not do

  • No account or sign-up, and no Folio servers that store your data.
  • No advertising, analytics, tracking or selling of data.
  • No access to your contacts, location or other apps' data.

Permissions the app uses

  • Camera: to scan documents and receipts. Pictures stay on your device.
  • Files and storage: to open files you choose, and to save copies and exports in Documents › Folio.
  • Fingerprint, face or screen lock (only if you turn on App lock): Android checks it; Folio never sees your fingerprint, face or PIN.
  • Internet: only for the optional Google Drive backup and, in the web app, to load the app itself (see below).

Reading text and scanning

In the Android app, text on receipts and documents is read on your phone with Google's ML Kit, and scanning uses Google's document scanner, which also runs on your phone. Your pictures aren't sent to us. Google may collect limited technical information about how these tools perform, as described in Google's own terms and privacy policy. In the web app, text is read inside your browser.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you turn it on, Folio saves your backups to a "Folio backups" folder in your own Google Drive. Folio asks only for permission to work with the files it creates there; it can't see the rest of your Drive. The backups belong to you and you can delete them in Google Drive at any time. Your use of Google Drive is covered by Google's privacy policy.

When you send or share

When you share, email or text a file (for example, an invoice to a client), Folio hands it to the app you pick, such as Gmail, WhatsApp or Messages. What happens next is governed by that app and the person you send it to. Folio doesn't keep a copy anywhere else.

The web app

The web app is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads some open-source parts (the PDF tools and the text reader) from cdnjs and jsDelivr. Like any website, those services receive normal technical information such as your IP address and browser type when the page loads. Your documents and receipts are not sent to them.

Keeping your information safe, and deleting it

  • Your information is protected by your device's own security. You can also turn on App lock in the Android app.
  • You can delete any document, receipt, invoice or record in the app.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing your browser's data for the web app, deletes what Folio stored. Copies in Documents › Folio, files you exported and backups you made are yours to keep or delete.
  • Because nothing is stored with us, we can't recover lost data. Please back up regularly.

Children

Folio is meant for adults managing their own documents and business records. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Not tax or legal advice

Tax names and rates in Folio are a starting point. Check them, and your receipt and invoice details, before relying on them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Important changes will also be mentioned in the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy: use File › Send feedback in Folio, or email (contact address coming soon).
